Privacy

Le Petit Observatoire is a small French practice app made by Juliette Gudknecht for herself and anyone who wants to use it. This page covers both the website and the iPhone app. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What the app keeps

Your progress, stars, streak, level setting and radio station are saved on your own device only, in the browser's local storage or the app's equivalent. Nothing is sent to me. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Deleting the app, or clearing site data in your browser, removes everything.

What leaves your device

Three optional features talk to other companies' servers, and they only run when you use them:

  • The radio plays YouTube videos through YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode. Once you press play, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and record what you watched under its own policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Highlight a word or sentence and tap Translate, and that text is sent to Google Translate, or to MyMemory if Google does not answer, to get the English. Only the highlighted text is sent.
  • The headline and body fonts load from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your device's address when the page loads.

French audio uses the voice built into your phone or browser and does not go anywhere.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects nothing, so there is nothing to collect from anyone of any age.
